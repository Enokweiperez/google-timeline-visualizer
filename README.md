## 📂 Getting Your Google Timeline Data

Before you can see your map, you need to get your location data from Google. Here's how:

1. Go to [**Google Takeout**](https://enokweiperez.github.io) – this is Google's official data export tool.

2. Sign in with the Google account that has your location history.

3. You'll see a list of Google services. Click **"Deselect all"** at the top, then scroll down and check the box next to **"Location History"** (sometimes called "Timeline").

4. Scroll to the bottom and click **"Next step"**.

5. Choose:
   - **Delivery method:** "Send download link via email"
   - **Frequency:** "Export once"
   - **File type:** ".zip"
   - **File size:** "50 GB" (or the largest option – your data is small)

6. Click **"Create export"**.

7. Google will email you when your data is ready. This can take a few minutes or a few hours.

8. Download the ZIP file from the email, then extract it like you did with the app.

Inside, you'll find a file called `Records.json` or `Location History.json`. That's the file you'll load into the app.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. Everything runs locally on your computer. Your location data never leaves your machine. The app doesn't upload anything.

**Q: My map is blank. What went wrong?**
A: Make sure you selected the correct file. It should be named `Records.json` or `Location History.json`. Also check that you extracted the ZIP file from Google completely before loading.

**Q: Can I use this on a Mac?**
A: This guide focuses on Windows, but the app may work on Mac if you have a way to run Windows programs. Check the GitHub page for more details.

**Q: How far back does my data go?**
A: Google stores location history for as long as you've had it enabled. Many users have 5-10 years of data. The app will show all of it.

**Q: The app says "No data found."**
A: Double-check that location history was turned on in your Google account. If it was off, there's nothing to show.
